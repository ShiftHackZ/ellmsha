## Changes

- Show and drag the desktop pet through nonactivating macOS panels, preserving the separate Dock action that opens the main window.
- Open command completion consistently at a valid slash invocation in either composer. Preserve surrounding text and caret position when selecting a command, handle empty results and input methods, and highlight known skills in the draft.
- Render BMAD document links and inline artifact buttons with readable Light/Dark colors, keyboard focus and disabled states inside their Shadow DOM.
- Keep finished or waiting subagents from becoming falsely active after a parent interaction. A genuine new child turn restores the running state; duplicate terminal events are suppressed.
- Fit subagent avatars within the available header width. Working children have animated circular borders; an ellipsis exposes the hidden children and their states. Reduced-motion preferences are respected.
- Automatically open a new Office document only when a live assistant reply in the exact current chat explicitly references it. Shared-workspace file events alone do not open unrelated spreadsheets. Existing files, historical messages and hidden content do not authorize opening; navigation cancels pending actions.
